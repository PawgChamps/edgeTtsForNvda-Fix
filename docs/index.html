Quick start

Ready in three steps

  1. 01Install

    Open the downloaded package and approve installation.

  2. 02Select

    Choose Edge TTS in NVDA or Android text-to-speech settings.

  3. 03Speak

    Pick a language and voice, then adjust rate and pitch to suit you.
